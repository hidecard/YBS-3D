YBS Ai 3D Map & GPS Guide

  • Zoom In / Out: Use the + / - buttons, mouse scroll wheel, or two-finger pinch on touchscreens.
  • Live GPS & Nearest Stops: Tap My GPS to show your live location pin on the 3D Yangon map and list the closest YBS bus stops.
  • Transfer Routing: Automatically finds Direct (0-transfer) and Multi-Transfer (1 or 2 bus changes) journeys with exact transfer stop names.
  • 3D Bus Stop Shelters: Tap any YBS bus shelter on the map to inspect lines or set your Origin/Destination.
